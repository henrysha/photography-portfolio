Behind the Lens

Meet Seong Wook Ha

Hi, I'm Seong Wook Ha, though most folks in the industry and behind the camera know me as Henry. I am an NYC-based photographer dedicated to capturing authentic moments, dynamic lighting, and unscripted human emotion.

My work spans live music concerts, artist portraits, wedding celebrations, travel photography, and candid street scenes. Whether I'm standing in a packed venue photo pit, directing an intimate portrait session, or capturing breathtaking travel landscapes, my philosophy remains constant: photograph the feeling, not just the subject.

Equipped with high-performance full-frame and medium-format gear, I bring technical precision and artistic intuition to every shoot—creating timeless images that resonate.
